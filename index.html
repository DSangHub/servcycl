Eatery plan

$10/month for 5 shift postings
$20/month for unlimited shift postings

Post up to 5 shifts before payment. Your first monthly payment is due after a worker first selects a shift, whether or not you confirm their placement. Those initial postings count toward your first month’s allowance. Further postings require payment once due.

Renews monthly from your first payment until canceled. Posting allowances reset each paid billing month; unused postings do not roll over. Worker earnings are paid separately. No per-shift service fee.
