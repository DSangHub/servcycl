Frequently asked questions

How does ServCycl work?

Create an account and save your worker profile with your ZIP code and earnings preference. Find a local shift that fits your schedule and select Accept shift; the eatery confirms your placement. Choose cash or food credits, or donate a portion of your earnings directly to a verified charity and receive the rest in cash. After you work the shift, the eatery can confirm it for your portfolio and add a rating.

How much should I donate?

Your choice. Even 5% of your check can make a difference.

How do I send my donation?

After you are paid, open Available and find the shift under My worked-shift portfolio. If your selected charity has a verified checkout link, select Donate and confirm the amount on the charity’s page.

How often should I donate?

As often as you like.

Do food credits expire?

Ask the eatery about its food-credit policy. Credits issued through the ServCycl wallet currently have no expiration; each offer also displays the eatery’s menu and usage rules.

Can I use food credits at another restaurant?

Participating eateries can agree to accept one another’s credits through the Co-Op meal-credit system. Open Food credits to see which eateries accept your specific credits. Acceptance is not automatic at every restaurant.

Can I gift food credits to a friend or family member?

Yes, when the issuing eatery allows gifting. Open Food credits, enter your friend or family member’s recipient member code, and choose the amount. They need a ServCycl account and must open their wallet once. Gifted credits keep the original eatery’s rules.
